Frequently Asked Questions

How do I authorize the add-on in Google Sheets™?

In Google Sheets™, open Extensions > YNAB Sync > Open Sidebar. If prompted, grant authorization for the script to access the current document. In the sidebar, click Connect to YNAB and authorize access to your budget data.

Why are some transactions marked as "Duplicate"?

The add-on creates deterministic import IDs based on transaction date, amount, and occurrence to prevent double imports. If YNAB already received that transaction, it safely flags it as duplicate rather than creating a duplicate entry in your budget.

Does the add-on store any of my bank or financial records?

No. The add-on is entirely serverless and runs client-side inside your Google Apps Script environment. All API requests travel directly between Google Sheets™ and YNAB's official servers.

How do I uninstall or revoke permissions?

Click Log Out inside the sidebar header or select Extensions > YNAB Sync > Log Out from YNAB, then go to Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons to uninstall. You can also revoke OAuth grants at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
